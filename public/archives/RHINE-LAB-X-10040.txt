HALL OF STASIS

Department: Remains Survey
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Investigation Team
Access: Reference material

The facilities and sealed devices left in the Hall of Stasis confront investigators with many unexplained phenomena. Incomplete information is itself a defining feature of the place. This volume preserves the site, its remains and unresolved questions rather than turning the unknown into a complete history without sufficient evidence.

Research notes
1. Record field observations separately from speculation about a device's purpose.
2. Preserve original descriptions even for objects that cannot be opened or explained.
3. Later evidence may supplement old records but should not erase the original questions.
