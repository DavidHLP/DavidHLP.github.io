A SHIFTING LABORATORY

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Trimounts Laboratory
Access: Reference material

The laboratory in Lone Trail has gravitational conditions that affect movement; mechanisms and movable objects together change how the space can be used. This volume studies such settings as facilities: when the floor is no longer a stable reference, routes, fixtures and operating habits all need reconsideration.

Research notes
1. Record safe positions for people and equipment before and after environmental changes.
2. Include moving objects' paths in arrangements for access and observation.
3. Provide clearly recognizable conditions of use for each state of the same space.
