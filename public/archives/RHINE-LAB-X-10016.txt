Rhine Lab's development cannot be separated from its external supporters. Investigations connect organizations such as the Maylander Foundation with its early growth. This volume examines how resources make research possible and how accepting support can change research goals and room for decision-making.

Research notes
1. Place funding, equipment and personnel support on the same project relationship map.
2. Record possible tensions between cooperation terms and research goals.
3. Discuss the use of results before their formal delivery.
