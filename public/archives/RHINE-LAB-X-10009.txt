MATERIAL MEMORY

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Engineering Section
Access: Reference material

Powered armor compresses materials, power and control systems into limited space. Rhine Lab's R-series equipment offers a way to examine these constraints. This volume studies material changes under repeated loads, asking whether equipment that appears normal again still carries traces of its previous use.

Research notes
1. Record external appearance separately from internal condition rather than relying only on visual inspection.
2. Compare maintenance needs of replaceable parts and overall load-bearing components.
3. Include fatigue, wear and repeated assembly in the same equipment history.
