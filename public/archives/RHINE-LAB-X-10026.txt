GREENHOUSE & HOME

Department: Ecological Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Muelsyse
Access: Reference material

Muelsyse's identity as an elf closely links environmental research to her own circumstances. The greenhouse is both a research site and a search for a suitable living space. This volume examines preservation and change: how can an environment that can be maintained become a place where life is truly possible?

Research notes
1. Include environmental needs and personal experience in observations.
2. Distinguish the conditions needed to preserve a specimen from those needed to sustain a way of life.
3. Research can explain environmental change but cannot decide a person's belonging for them.
