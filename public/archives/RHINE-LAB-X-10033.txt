ARCLIGHT ONE

Department: Energy / Joint Projects
Collection: Collected records across periods

Arclight One connects energy facilities, an aerial platform and multiple forces in Columbia. Its technical scale prevents any single section from completing the work alone. This volume examines conflicting goals in major engineering projects: the same equipment and resources may carry very different expectations for different participants.

Research notes
1. Compare alignment and divergence between research, military and personal exploration goals.
2. Understand critical decisions in light of the information participants had at the time.
3. Preserve concrete relationships between facilities and actions beneath the plan's name.
