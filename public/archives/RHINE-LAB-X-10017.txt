POINTS OF CONTACT

Department: Cooperation Affairs
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Dorothy / Rhodes Island
Access: Reference material

Dorothy takes part as the lead for a cooperative project between Rhine Lab and Rhodes Island. Institutional connections become concrete people, records and research tasks. This volume considers how cooperation builds trust and how limited, clearly defined collaboration can continue when the two sides hold different positions.

Research notes
1. Specify the scope of shared research and each side's responsibilities.
2. A personnel assignment provides communication channels, not complete agreement between institutions.
3. Cooperation should allow questions to be raised, not only display completed achievements.
