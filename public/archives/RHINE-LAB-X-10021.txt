R-SERIES PLATFORM

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Engineering Section
Access: Reference material

R-series powered armor appears in experimental facilities associated with Rhine Lab, where engineering equipment meets armed use directly. Starting from maintenance and operating conditions, this volume examines how complex equipment depends on cooperation and what new roles research results acquire after leaving the workbench.

Research notes
1. Distinguish equipment states during standby, maintenance and use.
2. Observe operators, maintenance personnel and the device as an interconnected system.
3. Record changes in use rather than explaining every later action through the original development goal.
