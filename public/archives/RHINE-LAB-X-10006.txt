LABORATORY SAFETY

Department: Defense Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Saria
Access: Reference material

Saria combines a scientific background with the ability to respond to abnormal incidents and formerly headed Rhine Lab's Defense Section. Her experience shows that laboratory safety means more than access controls and equipment: when project goals conflict with individual lives, someone must be able to stop the research and protect people on site.

Research notes
1. Preparation should include evacuation routes, contacts and conditions for stopping.
2. Incident records must explain who was affected, not only what equipment was damaged.
3. Security and researchers should share necessary information so section boundaries do not conceal risks.
