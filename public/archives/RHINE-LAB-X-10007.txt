INSTRUMENT CALIBRATION

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Mayer
Access: Reference material

Mayer prepared and tuned experimental equipment for a project involving Silence, and continues mechanical design at Lutra Workshop. Instruments turn researchers' ideas into measurable signals; errors in that process can be amplified by every calculation that follows.

Research notes
1. Preserve the correspondence between measurement references, equipment condition and raw readings.
2. When handing over equipment, also explain its applicable conditions and maintenance.
3. Local experience held by the people tuning equipment should become part of the project record.
