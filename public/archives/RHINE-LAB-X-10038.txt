LIMITS OF OBSERVATION

Department: High-altitude Research Records
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Rhine Lab
Access: Reference material

Knowledge of the sky has long been constrained by where observers can reach and what evidence they can obtain. Exploration in Lone Trail makes those limits open to discussion. This volume does not rush to name the unknown; it asks how a new observation forces old explanations to be tested again.

Research notes
1. Distinguish direct observations, inferences and hypotheses still awaiting verification.
2. When new material conflicts with existing explanations, preserve the evidence for both.
3. Record instruments and observation positions to explain the conditions limiting conclusions.
