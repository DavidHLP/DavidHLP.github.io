STRUCTURE & BOUNDARIES

Department: Engineering Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Engineering Section
Access: Reference material

Turning ideas from a laboratory into working facilities requires solving concrete problems of load-bearing, maintenance and access. Rhine Lab engineers provide equipment for large research projects. This volume examines the boundaries of facilities: who maintains an idea once built, and who bears the consequences of failure.

Research notes
1. Load-bearing structures and the life-science Structural Section address different questions.
2. Compare the maintenance-space requirements of experimental apparatus and facilities in continuous operation.
3. Preserve access for abnormal shutdowns rather than recording only normal equipment operation.
