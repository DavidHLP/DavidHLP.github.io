LONE TRAIL

Department: High-altitude Research Records
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Kristen Wright
Access: Reference material

Lone Trail carries Kristen's exploration beyond the sky and makes those on the ground reassess their relationship with her and Rhine Lab. This volume follows the trail of the event: each advance carries both continuing connections and farewells that cannot be undone.

Research notes
1. Compare actions in the sky with responses on the ground in chronological order.
2. Preserve different characters' understandings of the same moment.
3. Exploration matters both for arrival and for the questions it allows later people to ask.
