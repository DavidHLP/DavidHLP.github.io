THE DIABOLIC INCIDENT

Department: Historical Events
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Ifrit / Silence / Saria
Access: Reference material

The Diabolic Incident changed relationships between Ifrit, Silence and Saria and exposed deep problems in Rhine Lab's research. This volume centers its consequences, retaining threads of care, misunderstanding and responsibility; the later lives of those affected also belong in the record.

Research notes
1. Compare experimental records with subsequent experiences of care.
2. Identify missing information rather than filling every gap with one explanation.
3. Consequences appear in changed relationships and lives, not only damaged facilities.
