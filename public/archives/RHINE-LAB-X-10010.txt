OBSERVER’S JOURNAL

Department: Scientific Expedition Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Magallan
Access: Reference material

Magallan explores remote regions over long periods, repeatedly returning to the icefields for research. A field trip may bring back only an image or a specimen, yet it can remove a blind spot for the next researcher. This volume follows how field records accumulate into knowledge that can be passed on.

Research notes
1. Preserve routes, weather and observation positions to explain how the material was obtained.
2. List unexplained phenomena as questions for comparison in later observations.
3. Archives preserve wrong turns and unfinished surveys as well as successful discoveries.
