CARE IN TRANSIT

Department: Medical Cooperation
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Silence
Access: Reference material

Silence's medical drones are designed to deliver treatment. They extend care to places difficult for people to reach promptly, while remaining limited by environmental and equipment conditions. This volume asks how treatment is delivered and considers cooperation between medical tools and clinical judgment.

Research notes
1. Distinguish places equipment can reach from tasks it can actually complete.
2. Record field conditions, response time and subsequent care as a continuous process.
3. Tools extend doctors' abilities, but doctors must also judge when not to rely on them.
