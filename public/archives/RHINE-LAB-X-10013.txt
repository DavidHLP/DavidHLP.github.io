A SHARED BEGINNING

Department: Institutional History
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Kristen Wright / Saria
Access: Reference material

Public investigations trace Rhine Lab's early laboratory to the cooperation between Kristen and Saria. The founders shared ambitions but gradually formed different judgments. This volume places the institution's beginning beside their later choices to examine how shared ideals withstand real pressures.

Research notes
1. Trace development through the laboratory stage and the corporate organization stage.
2. Distinguish retrospective accounts from the information available at the time.
3. Co-founding an institution does not imply agreement on every later research project.
