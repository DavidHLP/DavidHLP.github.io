Ferdinand is closely connected to Rhine Lab's Energy Section and resource investment in major projects. Large energy engineering is therefore both scientific work and organizational cooperation. This volume explores the connections between efficiency, supply and external interests as energy moves from experimental results into practical use.

Research notes

2. At larger scale, a successful experiment cannot replace verification of sustained operation.
3. Project funding arrangements can also change the technical direction and use of results.
