SOARING DRAGON

Department: Research Equipment Development
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Magallan
Access: Reference material

Magallan's Soaring Dragon support drones use modular design to adapt to different tasks. Mapping and support needs meet on the same equipment platform. This volume examines the flexibility of modularity and the environmental and operational conditions that must be checked again when changing uses.

Research notes
1. Connect task requirements to module choices and record why changes were made.
2. Judge equipment capability alongside terrain, endurance and recovery conditions.
3. Do not conflate performance in different modes when one platform has several uses.
