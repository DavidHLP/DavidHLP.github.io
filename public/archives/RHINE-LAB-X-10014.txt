BETWEEN SECTIONS

Department: Control Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Rhine Lab
Access: Reference material

Rhine Lab works through several sections, with research, equipment, personnel and defense depending on one another. Silence's later change of position also reflects the connection between research decisions and organizational responsibility. This volume follows cross-section cooperation to trace how information moves within the institution and where it may stop.

Research notes
1. Distinguish formal responsibilities from temporary cooperation formed around major projects.
2. Handover records should describe unresolved questions as well as completed work.
3. When projects cross sections, make clear who can object and ask further questions.
