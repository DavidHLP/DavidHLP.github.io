CALCIFICATION STUDIES

Department: Originium Arts Records
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Saria
Access: Reference material

Saria's Arts concern calcium and its compounds and work together with her medical knowledge. Protection, support and environmental effects appear together in this ability. This volume uses it as an applied-research example, examining how purpose determines the use of the same technology.

Research notes
1. Connect an ability's effects to its purpose, targets and scope.
2. Compare the precision required for protection and medical support.
3. Do not judge the value of an Originium Art solely by its destructive power.
