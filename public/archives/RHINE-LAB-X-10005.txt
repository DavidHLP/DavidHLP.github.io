ECOLOGICAL OBSERVATION

Department: Ecological Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Muelsyse
Access: Reference material

The Ecological Section led by Muelsyse studies relationships between life and the environment and collaborates with Rhodes Island. A clean laboratory environment has particular significance for her. Starting with continuous observations of water and plants, this volume asks how much laboratory stability can explain changes in real environments.

Research notes
1. Compare water, plant and environmental records without treating correlation as causation.
2. Record sampling locations and conditions, preserving differences that cannot be reproduced in the laboratory.
3. Environmental purification affects both research results and the researcher's own needs.
