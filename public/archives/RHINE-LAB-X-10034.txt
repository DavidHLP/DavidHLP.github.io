RESONANCE DEVICES

Department: Originium Arts Applications
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Dorothy Franks
Access: Reference material

Dorothy specializes in applications of Originium Arts, and resonance devices offer a clue to her research direction. She hopes to improve others' circumstances, but good intentions do not establish whether a research method is appropriate. This volume places technical effects beside research motives and retains the questions between them.

Research notes
1. Record observable effects separately from the researcher's expectations.
2. Do not let an overall goal obscure participants' feelings and choices.
3. Clearly mark unknown mechanisms rather than presenting hopes as verified results.
