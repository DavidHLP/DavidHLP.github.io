COORDINATES OF TOMORROW

Department: Control Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Silence / Saria
Access: Reference material

After major events, Rhine Lab still has to decide where its next research should go. The scientific ethics discussion advanced by Silence adds another measure to that decision. This volume treats the future as an ongoing negotiation over research freedom, responsibility and cooperation rather than a finished answer.

Research notes
1. Compare technical goals with the actual lives they are intended to improve.
2. Leave room for different positions and record where consensus has not yet been reached.
3. After a project ends, continue following its long-term effects on people and the environment.
