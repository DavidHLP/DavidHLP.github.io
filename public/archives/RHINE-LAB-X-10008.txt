BEYOND THE HORIZON

Department: Control Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Kristen Wright
Access: Reference material

Kristen directed her research beyond Terra's sky. The exploration in Lone Trail turned questions about the heavens into an event involving all of Trimounts. This volume gathers observational clues while retaining the unresolved tension between ideals, concealment and cost.

Research notes
1. Examine the pursuit of the unknown separately from how specific projects are carried out.
2. Compare participants' judgments of the same event using the information available to them.
3. After a boundary is crossed, those left behind still face the consequences on the ground.
