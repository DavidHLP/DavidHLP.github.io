RHINE LAB, LLC.

Department: Control Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Kristen Wright / Saria
Access: Reference material

Rhine Lab grew from the laboratory co-founded by Kristen and Saria. It brings together researchers of life, energy and unknown technologies, while facing pressure from funding, power and the application of its results. Understanding the institution requires reading its experimental records alongside the choices of its researchers.

Research notes
1. Research spans several sections; major projects often depend on cooperation across them.
2. The technical value of a result and its eventual use need separate discussion.
3. Cross-reference institutional history and personal careers to preserve changes of position over time.
