LIFE IN TRIMOUNTS

Department: Regional Records
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Muelsyse
Access: Reference material

Trimounts is both the setting of scientific events and a city where researchers work, socialize and live. Muelsyse's records show interests and urban experiences beyond the laboratory. Through these everyday fragments, this volume observes a scientific city whose grand research plans are always carried out by individual people.

Research notes
1. Include living spaces alongside research spaces in a person's background.
2. People with different identities have different routes and memories in the same city.
3. Keep everyday details so people are not reduced to positions and achievements.
