IFRIT: RECOVERY

Department: Medical Cooperation
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Ifrit / Silence
Access: Reference material

Ifrit was a medical subject at Rhine Lab and was later cared for by Silence at Rhodes Island. Her story should not stop at unusual abilities or experimental experiences. This volume centers recovery, learning and everyday relationships, following how she comes to understand her power and build her own life.

Research notes
1. Record physical condition together with everyday adaptation rather than tracking abilities alone.
2. Preserve the person's own expression so observers' descriptions do not replace her voice.
3. Distinguish the effects of past events from changes happening now.
