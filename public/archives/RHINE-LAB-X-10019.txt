MEEBOO WORKBENCH

Department: Lutra Workshop
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Mayer
Access: Reference material

Meeboo is a mechanical device designed and operated by Mayer, as well as a creation she cares for. Maintenance, naming and keeping old parts gradually narrow the distance between tool and companion. From the workbench, this volume considers how creators understand the machines they make.

Research notes
1. Record the parts and experience retained during design iterations.
2. Include an operator's familiarity in equipment usage conditions.
3. Maintenance records also document how a creation gradually takes shape.
