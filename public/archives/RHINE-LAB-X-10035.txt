Events at Site 359 offer an important way to understand the connection between Dorothy's Vision and Lone Trail. Research inside the facility intertwines with pioneers' circumstances and outside interests. This volume organizes clues through participants and project relationships, avoiding explanations of everyone's motives derived only from the final outcome.
