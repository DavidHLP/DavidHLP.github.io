THE CONTOUR OF LIFE

Department: Structural Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Silence
Access: Reference material

Silence conducted life-science research involving Originium at Rhine Lab before becoming a medic at Rhodes Island. Her experience makes the distance between a research subject and a patient tangible: an apparently complete experimental result may still fail to explain how its subject will live.

Research notes
1. Observe tissue changes, functional changes and personal feelings within the same framework.
2. Cross-check clinical care records against the goals and assumptions of the experimental stage.
3. Preserve what remains unknown in research rather than hiding it behind an attractive conclusion.
