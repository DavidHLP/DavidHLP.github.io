SCIENCE & ITS PEOPLE

Department: Control Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Silence
Access: Reference material

Silence initiated the Trimounts Joint Declaration on Scientific Ethics and participates in related ethical affairs. She brought questions encountered in laboratories and clinical care into a wider discussion. This volume asks how individual people can retain a voice as science advances.

Research notes
1. Discuss the circumstances of research subjects, not only the benefits research may bring.
2. Record consent, disagreement and stopping as continuing processes.
3. Test rules repeatedly through concrete cases rather than treating written rules as final.
