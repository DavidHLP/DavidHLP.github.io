PTILOPSIS: RECORDS

Department: Medical Data
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Ptilopsis
Access: Reference material

Ptilopsis communicates in a distinctive way while continuing medical and records work. Her form of speech does not mean an absence of feeling. This volume examines information and human experience, reminding readers to understand the individual communicating with them beyond precise record formats.

Research notes
1. Record the time and conditions needed for communication rather than answering on the other person's behalf.
2. Understand expressive form separately from intent.
3. Reliable recordkeeping comes from sustained attention to details and individual needs.
