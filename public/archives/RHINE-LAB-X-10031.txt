DISTANT TRANSMISSION

Department: Energy Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Trimounts Project
Access: Reference material

The focus generators in Lone Trail are connected with ground-based energy transmission. As distance grows, transmitters, receivers and coordination together determine system performance. This volume follows the interdependence of the link's stages and treats interruption, deviation and recovery as equally important states to observe.

Research notes
1. Check transmission, reception and control signals within the same timeline.
2. When a link is interrupted, distinguish equipment problems from changes in external conditions.
3. Do not evaluate the whole transmission process only by the result that successfully arrived.
