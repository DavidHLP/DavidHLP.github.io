LIFE ON THE ICEFIELD

Department: Scientific Expedition Section
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Magallan
Access: Reference material

Magallan's icefield expeditions continually return distant knowledge to research institutions. In sparsely inhabited places, specimens, landforms and environmental conditions must be recorded together. This volume considers how to preserve field detail with limited information so later research can return to the place and circumstances of observation.

Research notes
1. Catalog specimens together with the environments where they were obtained.
2. Keep images, routes and descriptions for observations that cannot be repeated.
3. Remain open to unknown phenomena while stating how far the evidence supports conclusions.
