ORIGINIUM WORKFLOWS

Department: Equipment Development
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Mayer
Access: Reference material

Originium-related products bring energy applications into researchers' daily work. Mayer's records mention the importance she places on safe processing. This volume examines order at the workbench: how materials arrive, are processed and are handled afterward determines whether research can continue over time.

Research notes
1. Treat receipt, processing and recovery of materials as a continuous process.
2. When modifying equipment, also check whether existing protective conditions remain applicable.
3. Record practical difficulties so the next user can understand the procedure.
