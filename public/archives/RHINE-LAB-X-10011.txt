SIGNAL & NOISE

Department: Data and Records
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Ptilopsis
Access: Reference material

Ptilopsis maintained data at Rhine Lab and continues helping with medical records and information at Rhodes Island. Searchable data does not automatically become reliable knowledge. This volume examines the boundaries between recording, filtering and interpretation, especially the possibility that unusual signals are discarded as noise.

Research notes
1. Keep raw records alongside processed results so filtering criteria can be reconsidered.
2. Explicitly mark missing data and anomalies rather than filling them by default.
3. Interpret search conclusions within the circumstances in which the records were produced.
