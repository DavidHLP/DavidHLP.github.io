GALLERIA STELLARIA

Department: Observatory Records
Collection: Collected records across periods
Related people: Kristen Wright
Access: Reference material

Galleria Stellaria is an observation space designed by Kristen, with a central stellar projector presenting clues to the sky that invite further questions. It is both a facility and an expression of its designer's personal pursuit. This volume examines how spaces hold researchers' imaginations and how imagination approaches verifiable observation.

Research notes
1. Record the projector's presentation separately from observational material.
2. Read the facility layout alongside the designer's research goals.
3. A private place for stargazing may connect to plans far beyond an individual scale.
