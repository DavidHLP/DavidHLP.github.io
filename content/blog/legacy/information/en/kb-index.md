# Knowledge Base Index

This is the content-oriented entry point for the knowledge base. Read it before drilling into wiki pages and their `sources`.

The canonical wiki and raw sources are maintained in `zh-cn` so the LLM maintains one source of truth. Open the [canonical index](/blog/legacy/information/en/kb-index/), the [LLM-Wiki pattern](/blog/legacy/note/en/llm-wiki-pattern/), or the [ingest inbox](/blog/legacy/jotting/en/kb-ingest-todo/).

The maintenance contract lives in [KB.md](https://github.com/DavidHLP/DavidHLP.github.io/blob/main/KB.md) and the [knowledge-base skill](https://github.com/DavidHLP/DavidHLP.github.io/blob/main/.claude/skills/knowledge-base.md).

## Published translated pages

- [LLM-Wiki pattern](/blog/legacy/note/en/llm-wiki-pattern/) — the translated maintenance model for the knowledge base.
- [Headroom 0.34 compression and retrieval contract](/blog/legacy/note/en/headroom-compress-retrieve-contract/) — the explicitly translated API contract page.

## Canonical wiki

The complete concept and synthesis catalogue is maintained in `zh-cn`. Use the [knowledge base entry](/blog/legacy/information/en/kb-index/) for the canonical index and current page inventory.

## Entity pages

No translated entity pages are published. Personal projects, tools, frameworks, and runtime environments are added only after their sources and ownership are confirmed.

## Synthesis pages

No independent English synthesis pages are published. Read the canonical `zh-cn` pages through the knowledge base entry.

## Raw sources

Canonical raw evidence is maintained under `src/content/raw/zh-cn/` and is never exposed as a public route. The current source inventory is maintained in the [knowledge base entry](/blog/legacy/information/en/kb-index/).
